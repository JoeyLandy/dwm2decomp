DWM2 mod patcher

Apply a mod's .bps patch to your own copy of Dragon Warrior Monsters 2: Cobi's Journey (US). Everything happens in this page: no file leaves your computer.

The patch records the checksum of the ROM it was made from, so a different ROM (another revision, a ROM already patched) is refused rather than turned into a broken game. Patches are made with python -I tools/patch.py make in the dwm2decomp repository.
