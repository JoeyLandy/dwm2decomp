# Unidentified asset slices

The INCBIN slices of the reference ROM whose content is not identified (E-0383). Each was read from its loaders and rendered with tools/tile_sheet.py by a read-only drafter; the integrator checked a sample (for example that Map2FTileset decompresses 1,059 bytes and ends exactly where Map2FPalettes starts, so Gfx93_1 is its tail). The bytes are preserved either way; every other asset slice now carries a content name or a hand-written note in src/ (asset queue 0).

E-0387 removed the false slices: 28 streams found only by a chain of back-to-back streams (config/stream_chains.json) started inside a real stream or ran over the start of one, so the generator split real assets in two and turned the pieces into junk slices (eight of them were listed here as unidentified, and three more had been named as stream tails). tools/disasm_all.py now drops such chain streams, each real stream is one slice again, and tests/test_asset_streams.py checks that every compressed slice holds its whole stream. The bytes are unchanged.

Identified: 140 of the 153 slices E-0383 read; 8 were merged back into their streams by E-0387; unidentified: 5.
